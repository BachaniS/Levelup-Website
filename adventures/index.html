    // HERO MAKER

    MAKE IT LOOK LIKE YOU

    Take one selfie and the game reads it on the phone: skin tone, hair color and style, facial hair and glasses. Then it draws your pixel hero from what it saw. Every choice can be changed by hand.

    • Never leaves the phone. The photo is read on the device with Apple's Vision framework, then thrown away. It is never saved or sent anywhere.
    • Yours to tweak. 10 skin tones, 10 hair styles, 17 hair colors, 5 kinds of facial hair, glasses on or off.
    • Dressed by the shop. 24 pieces of armor, capes and trinkets show on your sprite.

    Eight heroes, all made in the Hero Maker. Tap one to swing.

    // ARCADE COMBAT

    EASY TO PICK UP. HARD TO PUT DOWN.

    Move with the stick and your hero attacks on their own. Your thumbs are free for the good part: dashing through danger, casting spells, and timing your class ability.

    • Perfect dodges. Dash through an attack and take no damage, plus a free crit and extra ultimate charge.
    • Combos. Every 5 hits in a row adds 1% damage, up to 20%. A heavy hit cuts it in half.
    • Fair fights. Armed enemies show a red warning before every swing, shields block from the front, and nobody hits you through a pillar.
    • Sideways or upright. Fights turn into a wide arena with console-style controls. Prefer portrait? One switch in Settings.
    • Training Grounds. A guided level teaches every move by having you do it. You can't lose, and the first finish pays 100 coins.
