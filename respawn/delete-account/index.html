Delete your account

Respawn, by Not Another Fitness App

You can delete your Respawn account and the data attached to it at any time. The quickest way is inside the app. If you can't get into the app, send us a request with the form below.

In the app

Open Profile and choose Delete account. This permanently erases your account and the data attached to it, and clears cached data on that device. It cannot be undone.

Without the app

This form goes to our support team at support@notanotherfitnessapp.com. Before we delete anything, we'll email the address on the account to confirm the request came from you.

Request sent We'll email the address on the account to confirm it's you before we delete anything. If you don't hear from us, write to support@notanotherfitnessapp.com.

If you used Sign in with Apple and chose Hide My Email, this address ends in @privaterelay.appleid.com. You can find it in your Apple Account settings under Sign in with Apple.

The name on your profile. It helps us find the right account.

What gets deleted

Your account and the data attached to it, including your profile, logs, stats, quests, scans and wallet.

What is kept

AI request logs. When you use an AI feature, we log the text of the request and the reply. These logs aren't linked to your account and contain no user ID, so they aren't part of the deletion. They expire on their own: the full text record after 3 days, and a reduced record (your prompt, timings and token counts, without the reply) after up to 180 days. See section 6 of the Privacy Policy.

Your subscription. Deleting your account doesn't cancel a paid subscription. Subscriptions are billed by Apple and managed in Settings, your Apple Account, Subscriptions. Cancel there if you no longer want to be charged.
