Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Respawn Adventures is an iPhone game from Not Another Fitness App, the makers of the Respawn fitness app ("we"). This policy covers the game. The Respawn app has its own Privacy Policy.

  • The game reads your Respawn stats. It never changes them.
  • Your selfie and your camera feed stay on your phone.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking. We don't sell your data.

1. Your Respawn Account

You can sign in with the same Apple, Google or email account you use in Respawn. Sign-in goes through Google Firebase Authentication. The game then reads your Respawn character from our Firebase database: your stats, class and level, and the training, sleep, meals, steps, streak, daily quests and XP behind them. It only reads this data and never writes to your Respawn account.

If you sign in with an Apple or Google account that has no Respawn account, the empty sign-in record that creates is deleted straight away. You can also play with a demo hero without signing in.

2. On Your Phone

Your game progress, coins, gear, settings and your hero's look are saved on your iPhone and aren't sent to us, except for the parts you share through the social features in section 4.

3. Camera and Photos

The Hero Maker reads one selfie, taken with the camera or chosen from your photos, with Apple's on-device Vision framework to suggest your hero's skin tone, hair, facial hair and glasses. Rep Power uses the camera to count squats or jumping jacks with on-device body tracking. In both, the image is processed on your phone and is never saved by the game or sent anywhere. Only the result is kept: your hero's colors and style, or a rep count.

4. Friends, Guilds and Parties

If you use the Social tab or Play Together, the game uses Apple's Game Center to identify you, to show which of your friends play (only with your permission), and to start online matches. To share guilds, parties and raid boards, it stores the following in the game's public database in Apple's iCloud (CloudKit), where other players of the game can see it:

  • your Game Center player ID and name
  • your hero's level, class and look
  • the guilds and parties you create or join, with their names, mottos and emblems
  • the raid damage you add each week

Leaving a guild or party removes you from it. To have everything else removed, email us.

5. Playing Together

In a live fight, your phone exchanges game data, such as your hero's moves, health and what is happening in the fight, with the other players' phones: through Game Center online, or directly over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi with phones in the same room. Nearby play identifies your phone with a random ID, not its name.

Room links (respawn-adventures-game.web.app/join/…) open a web page that shows the room code. It uses no cookies or analytics.

6. What We Don't Do

No advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, no selling or renting your data, and no sharing with data brokers. Apple's crash reports reach us only if you've chosen to share them with app developers in your iPhone's settings.

7. Service Providers

Google Firebase (sign-in, reading your Respawn data, and hosting the room-link page) and Apple (Game Center and iCloud) run parts of the game for us. They process data under their own terms and privacy policies.

8. Deleting Your Data

  • Game data on your phone: delete the app.
  • Guilds, parties and raid boards: leave them in the Social tab, or email us to delete everything stored about you in the game's iCloud database.
  • Your Respawn account: delete it in the Respawn app (Profile, then Delete account), as described in Respawn's Privacy Policy.
