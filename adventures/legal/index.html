Your data

Where each part of your data lives, and how to remove it.

  • Progress, coins, gear, settings and your hero's look These are saved only on your iPhone. Deleting the game deletes them.
  • Guilds, parties and raid boards These live in the game's iCloud database, where other players can see them. Leave a guild or party in the Social tab to take yourself off it. To have everything else removed, including your Game Center name and ID, your hero's level, class and look, and your raid damage, email us with your Game Center name and any guild or party codes.
  • Your Respawn account The game signs in with your Respawn account but never creates or changes one. Delete it in the Respawn app (Profile, then Delete account), or ask us to delete it.
