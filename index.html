QUESTS

A fresh board of bite-sized challenges every day, generated around your goals. Clear them for XP, chain streaks, and earn a daily letter grade.

  • Tiered difficulty. Every quest is T1–T10 — harder tiers pay out more XP.

  • Daily rating. Your day is scored S → F based on how much of the board you clear.
  • Unlock screen time. In Dedicated Mode, each quest banks 30 min of access to your blocked apps.

// DEDICATED MODE

EARN YOUR SCREEN TIME

Flip on Dedicated Mode and Respawn shields your distracting apps system-wide. Social media and games stay locked until you train — every quest you clear banks 30 minutes of access. Skip the board, stay locked.

  • Blocks social & games. Your chosen apps are shielded via native iOS Screen Time — not just a soft nudge.
  • 30 minutes per quest. Each completed quest banks a 30-min unlock. Clear more, earn more.
  • Resets every day. Earned time is scoped to today and never rolls over — a fresh board tomorrow.
  • You choose the apps. Pick exactly what to block from the system picker; flip back to Normal anytime.
