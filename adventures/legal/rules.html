Rules of Play

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Respawn Adventures turns the training you log in Respawn into a hero you fight with. These Rules explain how that works and how to keep the game fair for everyone, alone or with friends.

1. Your Hero Is You

Your hero's attributes, health, mana and daily buffs come from your Respawn character: the training, sleep, meals, steps and streak you log there. The game reads that data and never changes it. When your Respawn stats move, your hero follows.

2. Play Honestly

Logging training you didn't do in Respawn to power up your hero breaks Respawn's Rules of Play too. Don't modify the game, tamper with its save data, or use cheats, exploits or automation. In co-op, the host's game checks every move; don't try to get around it.

3. Coins, Gear & Rewards

Coins, weapons, gear, chests and raid rewards are earned by playing. They can't be bought, have no cash value, and can't be sold or traded. They may be rebalanced or reset, for example after a balance change or a detected exploit.

4. Names, Guilds & Parties

Guild and party names, mottos and emblems are seen by other players. Keep them free of hate, harassment, sexual content and slurs, don't impersonate anyone, and don't use names or marks you have no right to. We may remove a guild or party, or its name or motto, if it breaks these Rules. To report one, email support@notanotherfitnessapp.com with its code.

5. Play Together Kindly

Treat the people you play with as teammates. Don't use the game to harass, threaten or spam anyone. Share room codes and links only with people you want in your fight.

6. Move Safely

Rep Power counts real squats and jumping jacks. Clear the space around you, keep your phone somewhere stable, stay within your limits, and stop if anything hurts. See Health & Safety in the Terms & Conditions.

7. Fair Access

Don't reverse-engineer the game, interfere with Game Center, iCloud or our servers, or try to read or change other players' data or Respawn accounts. Breaking these Rules can mean losing shared records such as guilds and parties, or losing access to the game's online features.
